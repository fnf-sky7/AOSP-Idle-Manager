Policy guide

Default — AOSP handles the app normally.

Kill Background Process — runs am kill; Android kills only processes it considers safe to kill. The app may later restart.

Kill After 10 Minutes — after 10 minutes in Deep Doze, the module force-stops the app.

Full Kill — force-stops the app as soon as Deep Doze is detected. Android keeps it in the stopped state until a direct user/system launch clears that state; this is not a permanent autostart ban and does not disable/uninstall the package.

Safety: force-stop can interrupt notifications, alarms, accessibility, VPN, device automation and background sync. Some core Android packages are protected, but OEM-specific critical packages may not be recognized. Test policies one app at a time.
